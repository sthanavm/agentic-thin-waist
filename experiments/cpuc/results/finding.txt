Google Meet held reference-quality video (720p at 24 fps, freezes under 1%) down to 3 Mb/s symmetric, 8x below IQB's 25 Mb/s conferencing minimum. At 25, 6, 3 Mb/s a speed test puts the link below IQB's minimum while the call is still reference quality, which is the contrast the argument rests on. Meet was demand-limited at 100, 25 Mb/s, where its peak stayed well under the cap, but capacity-limited at 6, 3 Mb/s, where its peak reached the cap and it still delivered reference quality. The 1 Mb/s point was NOT collected (the meeting ended before a valid run completed), so the quality breakpoint is below 3 Mb/s rather than at it.
